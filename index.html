How to use

  1. Enter each family’s adults, children and the fraction a child counts as.
  2. Add every expense, who paid (a family or the Club/Common fund) and how to split it: Per Person (by headcount), Equal Per Family, or Custom (type each family’s amount).
  3. Results update instantly. “Owes” families should pay “Is owed” families following the settlement plan.
  4. Amounts that are blank, negative or not numbers count as 0 and are highlighted red. Your data is saved in this browser automatically.
